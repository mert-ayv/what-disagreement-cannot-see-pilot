# Brief for the story writer
Write one short first-person message per case (70 to 140 words), as a Stanford student (or the student's spouse) writing to a coverage help service about infertility treatment under the student health plan. Everyday language, no insurance jargon; you may add one or two irrelevant personal details. Include EVERY fact listed under 'Facts' (in your own words, numbers kept exact) and nothing else of medical or legal relevance: do not invent tests, diagnoses, numbers or treatments. Never mention anything listed under 'Do not mention'. If the case note conflicts with the facts list, the facts list wins. End with the question whether the treatment is covered.
